About

From diagnosis to the script that fixes the data

I have been building and implementing business management systems for more than fifteen years, and working with Sankhya ERP since 2016. Today my job is to make Sankhya handle the company's real process, with no side spreadsheets.

Today I am part of the Technology team at Hability Consult, a Sankhya-specialized consultancy with more than 100 active clients across 17 Brazilian states, and through TL Tec I serve companies in many industries with implementation, customization and integrations. I work closely with controlling, finance and billing teams. Before that, I was a tech lead at Sankhya itself and spent six years as a systems analyst in manufacturing, connecting production, tax and finance.
